# What makes the best TikTok viewer?

There are many "anonymous TikTok viewer" sites. They are not equal. Here is a checklist for judging one, and how
**[TTAnon](https://ttanon.com)** answers each point.

## The checklist

| Question | Why it matters | TTAnon |
|---|---|---|
| **Does it need an account or login?** | The point of a viewer is not signing in | No account, no sign-up, no login |
| **Does it load TikTok's scripts in your browser?** | Embedding TikTok's player or scripts brings its trackers along | TikTok's scripts, pixels and trackers never load on TTAnon pages |
| **Is it honest about what is not anonymous?** | Every viewer has limits; a good one tells you | Says plainly that video files play from TikTok's video servers ([details](https://ttanon.com/about#anonymous)) |
| **Does it use TikTok's URLs?** | You should be able to open any link you were sent | Same paths as TikTok: swap `tiktok.com` for `ttanon.com` |
| **Profiles, videos *and* photo posts?** | Many viewers break on slideshow posts | Videos and photo posts both |
| **Hashtags, sounds, topics, search, trending?** | Discovery without the app | All of them |
| **Video download?** | Saving a video should not need another site | MP4 download when the creator allows it |
| **Real stats with dates?** | Counts without a date are guesses | Follower / like / video counts with their date, follower history, [method documented](https://ttanon.com/about/stats) |
| **More than TikTok shows?** | A viewer can add value, not just mirror | Transcripts, location, language, resolution, sound album and streaming links, interaction settings |
| **Tools for analysis?** | Comparing a creator's videos | Filter, sort, chart by month and CSV export on profiles |
| **Fast on a phone?** | Most people open TikTok links on mobile | Server-rendered pages served from a CDN; layout does not jump while loading |
| **Pop-ups, app banners, login walls?** | They are why people look for a viewer in the first place | None of TikTok's |
| **Respects creators?** | Content belongs to its creators | Public content only, no private accounts, [removal / DMCA process](https://ttanon.com/dmca) |
| **Independent?** | You should know who you are dealing with | Independent, not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance, [contact page](https://ttanon.com/contact) |
